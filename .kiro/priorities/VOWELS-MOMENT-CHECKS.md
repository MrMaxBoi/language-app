# Vowels — Moment Checks (phone test sheet)

_For Today item 4, the physical-phone test. These are NOT pool questions and are
NOT seeded. They check the acceptance outcome by hand: after the lesson, can the
learner understand **あお** or **うえ** in a visual situation, without leaning on
romaji?_

**How to run:** do these on the device straight after a learner finishes the
Vowels lesson, before any review. Show the art, say nothing in Japanese, and
watch what they do. No romaji is shown or spoken by the tester.

**Overall pass bar:** 2 of 3 on the first attempt, with no romaji spoken aloud by
the learner. If they sound out "u-e" or "a-o" to decode, that attempt is a soft
fail even if the final tap is right — note it.

---

## Check 1 — The balloon (うえ, visual only)

- **Show:** the rooftop illustration, balloon high above Hana. No text on the art.
- **Ask (in English):** "Two words. Tap the one that says where the balloon is."
- **Offer:** two cards, `うえ` and `あお`, no romaji, no English.
- **Pass:** taps `うえ` within about 5 seconds, without sounding out romaji.
- **Why it tests the outcome:** direction word in a real scene, with the colour
  word as the tempting rival (the sky is blue *and* the balloon is up).

## Check 2 — The sky (あお, visual only)

- **Show:** the blue-sky illustration (one white cloud).
- **Show prompt card:** `あお` (no romaji).
- **Offer:** three colour swatches — blue, red, green — no labels.
- **Pass:** taps the blue swatch on the first try.
- **Why it tests the outcome:** reads the word cold and maps it to a real colour,
  not to an English gloss on screen.

## Check 3 — The spoken moment (あお vs うえ, audio + visual)

- **Show:** the sea-at-dusk image. **Play:** Grandma's line `あお`.
- **Offer:** two word cards, `あお` and `うえ`, no romaji.
- **Ask (after they tap, in English):** "What does it mean?"
- **Pass:** picks `あお` AND answers "blue" (not "up", not "sky-direction").
- **Why it tests the outcome:** joins the heard word, the written word and the
  meaning in one real moment — the whole acceptance outcome in miniature.

---

## Recording the result

For each check note: tapped right/wrong, first try y/n, romaji spoken y/n, and
time-to-tap if it felt slow. A learner who passes 2 of 3 silently has met the
acceptance outcome. A learner who only passes by sounding out romaji has not —
that points back at the lesson, not the quiz.
